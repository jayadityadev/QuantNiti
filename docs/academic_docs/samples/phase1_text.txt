ABSTRACT
The rapid growth of financial markets and the increasing participation of retail investors have
created a strong demand for intelligent tools that can assist in investment analysis and decisionmaking. Traditional investment approaches often rely on intuition or limited market analysis, which
may lead to higher financial risks and poor strategy selection. To address these challenges, this
project presents an Indian Market Portfolio Intelligence and Back testing Platform, a machine
learning–based system designed to evaluate trading strategies using historical stock market data.

The proposed platform integrates portfolio analysis, strategy back testing, market regime detection,
and risk assessment into a unified framework. Historical market data is collected dynamically using
the Yahoo Finance API and processed to generate important financial indicators such as returns,
volatility, momentum, and maximum drawdown. The system supports multiple predefined trading
strategies including Buy and Hold, Moving Average Crossover, RSI-based trading, Momentum
Strategy, and Mean Reversion Strategy. These strategies are tested against historical market
conditions to evaluate their effectiveness and performance.

Machine learning techniques are incorporated to improve strategy analysis and recommendation.
Unsupervised learning methods are used for market regime detection, enabling the identification of
bullish, bearish, and sideways market conditions. Supervised learning models are employed to
predict the suitability of trading strategies under varying market scenarios. In addition, the platform
performs risk and performance analysis by calculating important financial metrics such as Sharpe
Ratio, volatility, drawdown, and cumulative returns.
The system is designed to benefit investors, researchers, and students by providing data-driven
insights into stock market behaviour and trading performance. By combining financial analytics
with machine learning, the platform enhances investment decision support and promotes a better
understanding of portfolio management and risk evaluation in the Indian stock market environment

Chapter 1
INTRODUCTION
The stock market plays a major role in the global economy by enabling individuals and
organizations to invest, trade, and grow their financial assets. In recent years, the Indian stock
market has experienced rapid growth due to increased participation from retail investors, digital
trading platforms, and the availability of real-time financial data. However, financial markets
are highly dynamic and unpredictable, making investment decisions difficult for both beginners
and experienced investors. Selecting an appropriate trading strategy without proper
Traditionally, investors rely on technical indicators, historical price charts, or market intuition
to make trading decisions. While these methods provide useful insights, they often fail to
evaluate the robustness of a strategy under different market conditions. Existing platforms
mainly focus on visualizing stock prices rather than analysing how trading strategies perform
during bullish, bearish, or sideways markets. As a result, there is a need for a more intelligent
and data-driven system that can evaluate strategies, measure risks, and provide meaningful
investment insights.
To address these challenges, this project proposes an Indian Market Portfolio Intelligence and
Back testing Platform that combines financial analytics with machine learning techniques.. It
supports multiple trading strategies such as Buy and Hold, Moving Average Crossover, RSIbased trading, Momentum Strategy, and Mean Reversion Strategy.
The system retrieves historical stock market data from Yahoo Finance and processes it to
generate important financial features such as returns, volatility, momentum, and drawdown.
Machine learning algorithms are integrated into the platform to detect market regimes and
recommend suitable strategies based on prevailing market conditions. The project also
performs portfolio analysis and risk assessment using important performance metrics like
Sharpe Ratio, cumulative returns, and volatility measures.
The proposed platform serves as a valuable tool for investors, financial researchers, and
students interested in stock market analytics. By integrating strategy evaluation, machine
learning, and risk analysis into a single system, the project aims to improve investment
decision-making and provide a deeper understanding of financial market behavior in the Indian
stock market environment.

METHODOLOGY
The proposed Indian Market Portfolio Intelligence and Backtesting System is a regime-aware
AI-driven financial analytics platform designed to evaluate trading strategies under different
market conditions using machine learning and portfolio analysis techniques. The system
integrates market regime detection, historical backtesting, strategy evaluation, and risk
forecasting into a unified framework for Indian equity markets

Fig: 6.1 Architecture Diagram
6.1 Data Collection
Historical stock market data is collected using the Yahoo Finance API through the yFinance
library. The system retrieves OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume) data for selected
stocks and indices based on the user’s preferred time range.
6.2 Data Processing
Raw market data may contain missing values, duplicate records, or inconsistencies. Therefore,
preprocessing is performed to improve the quality and reliability of the dataset.
The preprocessing stage includes:
•

Handling missing or null values

•

Removing duplicate records

6.5. Trading Strategy Implementation
The platform implements multiple trading strategies that are commonly used in stock market
analysis. Each strategy follows a different trading logic and investment approach.
The strategies include:
•

Buy and Hold Strategy

•

Investors purchase stocks and hold them for a long period regardless of short-term
market fluctuations.

•

Moving Average Crossover Strategy

•

Buy and sell signals are generated when short-term moving averages cross long-term
moving averages.

•

RSI Strategy

•

The Relative Strength Index is used to identify overbought and oversold conditions in
stocks.

•

Momentum Strategy

•

Stocks with strong recent performance are selected with the expectation that the trend
will continue.

•

Mean Reversion Strategy

•

This strategy assumes that stock prices eventually return to their historical average
values.

•

Each strategy is tested independently using historical market data.

6.6 Backtesting Process
Backtesting is used to evaluate how a trading strategy would have performed in past market
conditions. The platform simulates real-time trading using historical stock data.
During backtesting, the system:
•

Generates buy and sell signals

•

Executes virtual trades

•

Tracks profits and losses

•

Calculates portfolio growth over time

The backtesting engine helps determine whether a strategy is profitable, stable, and suitable for
different market environments.
