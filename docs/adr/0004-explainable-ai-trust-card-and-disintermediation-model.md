# Explainable AI Trust Card and Disintermediation Moat

**Status:** accepted

## Context
Black-box machine learning systems face severe skepticism in finance. Retail investors frequently ask: *"Why should I trust an algorithm over a human bank relationship manager?"* Traditional wealth advisors charge 1.5%–2.5% in ongoing AUM fees while often pushing high-commission financial products with inherent conflicts of interest.

## Decision
Every portfolio basket and stock intelligence recommendation is accompanied by an **Explainable AI (XAI) Trust Card** resting on four verifiable pillars:
1. **Regime Context**: Active macro economic state (GMM clustering).
2. **Historical Hit Rate**: 5-year rolling out-of-sample directional prediction accuracy.
3. **Stress Drawdown Limit**: Maximum peak-to-trough historical drawdown guardrail.
4. **Disintermediation Fee Savings**: Direct cost savings calculated by eliminating the 1.5%–2.5% wealth management intermediary fee.

## Consequences
- Replaces opaque AI predictions with transparent, verifiable probabilistic risk boundaries.
- Provides retail users with an explicit mathematical justification for adopting algorithmic self-direction.
- Serves as a primary defense asset for academic viva and panel presentations.
