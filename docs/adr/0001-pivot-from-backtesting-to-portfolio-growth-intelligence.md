# Pivot from Technical Backtesting to Portfolio Growth Intelligence

**Status:** accepted

## Context
During Project Phase 1 evaluation, the academic review panel highlighted that non-finance retail investors find technical indicator strategies (Moving Average Crossover, RSI Mean Reversion, Bollinger Bands) impenetrable. Retail users asked direct, practical questions: *"Where should I invest?", "How much will my capital grow?",* and *"Why should I trust this over a bank?"*

## Decision
We pivoted the core product value proposition from an indicator-based strategy backtester for active day traders to an AI-driven Portfolio & Stock Growth Intelligence platform for retail investors. The system preserves our underlying machine learning forecasting and regime detection engines while translating quantitative outputs into accessible, probabilistic growth projections and transparent risk guardrails.

## Consequences
- Retains mathematical and econometric rigor while dramatically improving accessibility.
- Aligns product features with SEBI educational guidelines rather than speculative intraday signals.
- Established the foundational requirement for the Explainable AI Trust Card.
