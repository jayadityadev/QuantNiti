# Interactive Financial Literacy Hub & Playable Video Explainer Architecture

**Status:** accepted

## Context
India's retail financial literacy rate is 27% (NCFE-FLIS). Introducing advanced quantitative methodologies (Hierarchical Risk Parity, GMM regime clustering, Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage) to beginners without contextual education leads to confusion and mistrust. Text-only tooltips are frequently skipped.

## Decision
We implemented a structured, multi-modal **Learning Hub** on the Home tab:
1. **4 Structured Curriculums**: Organized by difficulty (*Basics*, *Regimes*, *Risk*, *Quant*), with everyday analogies explaining mathematical concepts.
2. **Playable 16:9 Video Explainer Facades**: Responsive embedded YouTube video guides (`youtube-nocookie.com`) paired with quantitative takeaways and difficulty ratings.
3. **Progressive Inline Links**: Contextual "Learn" links placed adjacent to algorithmic metrics throughout the Explore, Grow, and Portfolio tabs.

## Consequences
- Transforms QuantNiti into a dual investment intelligence and financial education platform.
- Enhances panel defense by demonstrating measurable social impact in financial literacy.
- Embeds video media securely without third-party tracking cookies.
